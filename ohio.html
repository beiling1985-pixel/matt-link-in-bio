What "living benefits" means

Most people think life insurance only pays when you die. Many policies today can also pay part of the benefit early if you're diagnosed with a qualifying critical, chronic or terminal illness, like a heart attack, stroke or cancer.

That money can go toward your mortgage, your bills, or anything else your family needs while you recover. What's covered and how much pays out depends on the carrier and the plan, and Matt will show you the details before you decide.
