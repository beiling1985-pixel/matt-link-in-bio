Privacy Policy

Beiling Agency · Matt Beiling · Effective October 22, 2026

This policy covers the Beiling Agency website and the ICL Agent Runtime, the private software Beiling Agency uses to run its own office (appointment reminders, scheduling and internal records). The ICL Agent Runtime is used only by Beiling Agency. It is not offered to the public.

Information we collect

  • Website forms. If you request a quote or contact us, we collect what you enter (such as your name and contact details) so we can reply.
  • Google account data (agency owner only). When the agency owner connects his own Google account to the ICL Agent Runtime, it accesses:
    • Google Calendar: events on calendars the owner owns, to see booked appointments, send appointment reminders and keep event titles consistent.
    • Gmail (read-only): the owner's inbox, to find insurance carrier contracting emails. It cannot send, delete or change email.
    • Google Docs: documents the software uses as its working notes.

How we use it

We use this information only to run Beiling Agency's own work: confirming and reminding clients about appointments they booked, tracking recruiting paperwork, and keeping internal notes. We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. Mobile numbers and text-message consent collected on our forms are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for their marketing. If you ask for information on our site, Matt or his assistant (which may be an AI assistant) may text or call you about the coverage you asked about.

Google user data

The ICL Agent Runtime's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only for the features described above. It is not transferred to others except as needed to provide those features, to comply with law, or with the owner's consent. It is not used to train general AI models, and people do not read it except when needed for security, to comply with law, or with the owner's permission.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run these tools: Close (our CRM and texting), Render (hosting), Google, and AI providers that process text on our behalf to draft replies and summaries. They handle data only to provide their service to us.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted on our hosting provider and are used only by the software. You can ask us at any time what we hold about you.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text from us to stop texts.
  • Email us to ask for your information to be corrected or deleted.
  • The agency owner can remove the software's Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
